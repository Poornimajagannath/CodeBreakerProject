2. Morning routine

  1. Cleanse lightly
    Lukewarm water rinse, or gentle non-foaming cleanser if oily. No hot water; no AM double cleanse unless very oily.
  2. Ranavat Brightening Saffron Serum
    2–3 drops on damp face and neck.
  3. Optional hydrating serum
    Only if you already use a second Ranavat hydrator.
  4. Eye cream
    Live Tinted Superhue Brightening Eye Cream.
  5. SPF — do not skip
    Hueguard 3-in-1 SPF 30. Longer outdoor days: Skin Tint SPF 50. Reapply every 2 hours outdoors.
  6. Makeup (optional)
    Skin Tint / concealer after SPF settles. Keep mouth/cheek products non-comedogenic.
Cleanse→ Ranavat→ Eye→ Hueguard SPF→ Makeup

3. Night routine

Tretinoin nights (3–4×/week to start)

  1. Gentle cleanse
    Fully remove SPF/makeup. No scrubs.
  2. Wait until skin is fully dry
    About 15–20 minutes — reduces sting.
  3. Apply tretinoin
    Pea-size for the full face. See barrier sandwich below if sensitive.
  4. Moisturizer
    Seal with plain ceramide moisturizer.
On tret nights avoid: Superbright vitamin C stick, AHAs/BHAs, strong brightening sticks on raw skin, and retinoid eye cream near the eyes.

Calm-skin layering (optional)

If skin is calm and not stinging: Ranavat saffron serum → wait → tretinoin → moisturizer.

Recovery nights (1–3×/week, or whenever dry/flaky)

  1. Cleanse
  2. Ranavat serum(s)
  3. Richer moisturizer / barrier cream
  4. No tret, no acids, no vitamin C stick

4. Barrier sandwich protocol

Use this whenever you notice dryness, flaking, stinging, or mouth-area irritation while on tretinoin.

  1. Cleanse and dry fully
    Same 15–20 minute wait.
  2. Thin moisturizer first
    Plain ceramide cream as a buffer layer.
  3. Pea-size tretinoin
    Apply over the buffer; keep away from lips, nostrils, and immediate eye area.
  4. Moisturizer again
    Seal. Optional plain petrolatum only on dry patches — not on active bumps.
Moisturizer→ Tretinoin→ Moisturizer
If irritation continues after sandwiching: drop to every-other-night or 2–3×/week until calm, then rebuild.
Stop and see a dermatologist for burning, widespread rash, or worsening perioral bumps.

5. Four-week schedule

Default calendar assumes tret on Mon / Wed / Fri / Sun in weeks 1–2, then add nights only if skin stays calm.

PM: cleanse → dry → sandwich tret → moisturizer

Recovery
Tue · Thu · Sat

AM: Ranavat → Hueguard
PM: cleanse → Ranavat → richer moisturizer · no tret

Weeks 3–4 — increase only if calm

  • No stinging or peeling for 5+ days? Add one extra tret night (e.g. Mon–Fri + Sun).
  • Still dry or bumpy near the mouth? Stay at 3–4×/week and keep sandwiching.
  • Take same-lighting progress photos at day 1 and day 28.

Weekly rules

  • No physical exfoliation while on tret.
  • No new actives for these 4 weeks — let tret handle texture.
  • Do not pick mouth-area bumps; use barrier care instead of extra acids.
  • Vitamin C / hyperpigmentation sticks: AM only, or non-tret nights.
  • Hueguard every morning — highest-ROI Live Tinted step with tret.

6. What not to change

  • Keep Hueguard daily.
  • Keep Ranavat serum as the glow/hydrate layer and tret buffer.
  • Keep tretinoin as the only strong cell-turnover active.
